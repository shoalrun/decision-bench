Each report records a comparison against fixed questions with known answers. Reports retain the exact model versions, dataset, settings, and results so comparisons remain understandable as the model catalog grows.

Development cases check the evaluation’s prompts and runner settings; held-out test cases measure performance after those choices are fixed. Neither set trains the models.

| Evaluation date | Models | Case coverage | Report |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 30 October 2026 | Mammoth 1, Liquid d1.1, Jev 1.14, Elephant 1, Joey 1 | 496 unique cases; three formats on 400 | [Executable known-answer tasks](doc:report.2026-10-30) |

The 30 October report was updated on 31 October to include Liquid’s matching-Python-client results while retaining the original four-model measurements.

See [methodology](doc:guide.methodology) for how the runner obtains known answers, or [add an evaluation report](doc:guide.add-report) to record another comparison.
